# Contributing to DME_Lab

DME_Lab is currently developed under a source-available, noncommercial public
license with separate commercial licensing reserved by the repository owner.

Because accepting third-party copyrighted contributions can fragment the rights
needed to maintain that dual-licensing structure, **external code,
documentation, schemas, tests, or other copyrightable contributions are not
accepted for inclusion unless the contributor has first agreed to separate
written contribution terms supplied or approved by the repository owner.**

## What is welcome without a contribution agreement

You may still open issues or discussions that:

- report bugs;
- describe observed behavior;
- point to relevant public research;
- suggest experiments or pressure cases;
- identify security or correctness problems; or
- discuss ideas and methods.

Do not paste substantial third-party code, proprietary material, or content you
do not have the right to share.

An issue, discussion, review comment, or other informal message does not by
itself grant the repository owner commercial relicensing rights to
copyrightable material contained in that message.

## Pull requests

Do not submit a pull request containing material intended for incorporation
unless the repository owner has invited the contribution and the applicable
contribution terms have been agreed.

Unsolicited pull requests may be closed without incorporation.

When outside contributions are accepted in the future, the required
contribution agreement should preserve enough rights for the project owner to:

- distribute the contribution under the repository's public license;
- include it in separately licensed commercial versions;
- sublicense or relicense it as needed for project distribution; and
- maintain consistent licensing across future versions.

The exact contributor agreement, if any, must be supplied separately. This file
is not itself a copyright assignment or contributor license agreement.

## Contributor ownership

Nothing in this policy claims ownership of a contributor's pre-existing work.

Any future contribution agreement should state explicitly what rights the
contributor retains and what rights are granted to the project owner.

## Boundary

```text
PUBLIC RESEARCH PARTICIPATION
!=
AUTOMATIC SOFTWARE CONTRIBUTION

CONTRIBUTION ACCEPTED
!=
COMMERCIAL RELICENSING RIGHT
```

Until a separate contribution agreement closes that gap, copyrighted outside
contributions should not be merged.
