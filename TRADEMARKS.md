names, logos, or branding as a source identifier for another product or service.

include:

- DME;
- DME_Lab;
- ATLAS;
- Concordance; and
- associated logos, visual identifiers, and branding.

This policy applies only to rights that are protectable and owned by the
repository owner. It does not claim that every listed word is registered,
exclusive in every field, or free of third-party rights.

## Permitted referential use

Truthful, non-misleading reference to the project is permitted to the extent
allowed by law, for example:

- stating that research was inspired by or compared with DME_Lab;
- identifying an unmodified copy or a fork of DME_Lab;
- linking to the original repository; or
- accurately describing compatibility or provenance.

Such use must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, official status, or
affiliation that does not exist.

## Uses requiring separate permission

Separate permission is required before using protected project branding, to the
extent such rights exist, in ways likely to identify another product, company,
commercial service, distribution, or hosted offering as originating from or
being officially affiliated with this project.

Examples include:

- naming a commercial product or service "DME", "DME_Lab", or "ATLAS" in a way
  that implies official project origin;
- using project logos as the primary branding for another distribution;
- registering confusingly similar branding for a commercial fork or hosted
  service; or
- describing an independent fork as "official".

Forks and independent implementations should identify themselves clearly as
independent and unofficial unless written permission says otherwise.

## No software-right expansion

Permission to use a project name does not grant software, patent, commercial,
or contribution rights.

Likewise:

```text

!=
```
