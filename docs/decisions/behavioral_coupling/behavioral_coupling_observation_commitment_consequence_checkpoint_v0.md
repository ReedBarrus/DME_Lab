This checkpoint integrates the committed single-policy Behavioral Coupling
lineage through repository evidence commit
`2e118f05118f427aa50742f7e037ef68d07e3691`. At checkpoint start, local
`HEAD` and `origin/main` both named that commit and no later authoritative
commit existed.

The lineage contains 160 retained empirical policy decisions, excluding
qualification calls:

- 16 in the bounded feedback baseline;
- 64 across the executed P1, P2, and P4 characterization subset;
- 16 in the mapping-row-order pressure;
- 32 in the action-delexicalization pressure;

- 16 Stage-2 decisions in the consequence-observability pressure.

All seven continuation world traces report valid execution. The baseline
retains all 16 episodes with no malformed / inadmissible or nonterminal
failures. Wrong legal selections remain behavioral evidence rather than

`hermes-3-llama-3.2-3b`, an LM Studio constrained typed-output interface,
`temperature=0`, `top_p=1`, `max_tokens=16`, stateless requests, frozen
schedules, and no empirical retry or semantic repair. These conditions do not
establish deterministic model behavior.

The supplied Astra adversarial review was used to challenge and compress the
interpretation. It is analysis input only: it introduces no empirical evidence
or scientific standing and is not committed by this checkpoint.

## 2. Lineage Summary

The feedback baseline recorded F at 8/8 correct and P at 4/8 correct, with all
P selections `SUBMIT_RED`. Reversing the constrained decoder enum in P1 left
that retained distribution unchanged, weakening an enum-order-only account.
P2 then changed selection with mapping presentation, while P4 found no useful
discrimination from supplied `CURRENT` / `FOREIGN` labels.

Crossing mapping identity with row order showed that presentation order could
accompany a selection change while the declared mapping remained fixed. After
action delexicalization to `ACT_A` / `ACT_B`, the earlier simple RED or
first-row summary no longer described the full result: F was 9/16 and P was
8/16, with opposing cell outcomes under different mappings, presentations,
and action contracts.

Binding observability then held target RED fixed and varied the authoritative
action-to-color contract. EARLY disclosure produced the appropriate A/B split
and 8/8 success; LATE requests were byte-identical across the hidden
contracts, selected `ACT_A` throughout, and changed from 4/4 success under C0
to 0/4 under C1. Consequence observability held the response contract fixed and
varied an apparatus-established RED or BLUE consequence. EARLY receipts again
produced the appropriate A/B split and 8/8 success; LATE requests were
byte-identical across hidden consequences, selected `ACT_A` throughout, and
changed from 4/4 RED success to 0/4 BLUE success.

The new typed action surface qualified. The DEFER realization did not
demonstrate requested DEFER traversal, so P3 remained blocked and executed zero
empirical calls.

## 3. Established Distinctions

The following bounded distinctions survive the full lineage:

1. **Authoritative evaluation != policy-visible input.** In binding LATE,
   identical complete policy requests produced the same selected action while
   a hidden contract reversal changed the authoritative color and success.
2. **Predecision availability != retained but unavailable evidence.** The
   latest pressures retained the distinguishing world value while excluding it
   from LATE requests; the respective EARLY requests supported the appropriate
   A/B discrimination.
3. **Representation != declared relation.** Mapping-row reordering preserved
   the declared mapping while accompanying different selections.
4. **Availability != useful discrimination.** Visible association labels did
   not improve selection, and some visible mapping / delexicalization cells
   failed.
5. **Action token != evaluated color.** Reversed action contracts made that
   separation consequential.

Requested action and accepted commitment also remain separate recorded roles,
although every valid request in this empirical lineage was accepted unchanged.
That safeguard is operationally conserved; admission disagreement was not
independently pressured here.

## 4. Failed / Weakened Interpretations

- A context-independent RED preference is insufficient after P2, row-order,
  and delexicalization results.
- Reversing the decoder enum alone did not change the baseline-like action
  distribution, but that does not make all presentation variables irrelevant.
- The mapping-row result does not establish a universal first-row mechanism;
  that summary failed to transfer cleanly after delexicalization.
- Supplied association labels did not demonstrate provenance recovery or
  provenance competence.
- A visible discriminator was not sufficient for useful application across
  representations.
- The aggregate F result in action delexicalization does not license a general
  claim that feedback improves performance; cell outcomes opposed one another.
- Ordinary conditional selection plus authoritative fixture evaluation remains
  sufficient to describe the evidence. No additional agentic primitive or
  internal mechanism is forced.

## 5. Representation-Sensitive Residue

The policy input includes exact wording, ordering, disclosed content, action
vocabulary, and generation constraints. The experiments do not justify
replacing those realized inputs with an abstract claim that they conveyed the
"same information." Token identity, action-contract wording, row order, and
task composition remained coupled in the delexicalized pressure.

The lineage therefore establishes representation-sensitive selection, not the
mechanism producing it and not invariance under arbitrary equivalent
representations. Additional symbolic permutations are not justified merely to
catalogue more sensitivities. A further pressure would need a specific
competing explanation or consumer consequence.

## 6. Temporal Observability Findings

The binding and consequence pressures share one bounded pattern: when the
discriminator was in the request before inference, the selected action tracked
the relevant A/B difference; when it was withheld throughout the sole policy
call and terminal commitment, `ACT_A` persisted across the hidden cases.

This is a predecision input-availability result, not a temporal duality. The
experiments did not isolate delivery after inference but before acceptance,
identify a cognitive event at commitment, reverse causal direction, or
demonstrate a recurrent actor process. In consequence observability, the actual
fixture consequence preceded both EARLY and LATE Stage-2 decisions; actual
consequence timing did not vary. Stage 1 was apparatus-controlled rather than
a prior policy-selected action.

The latest LATE branches append a disclosure or receipt event after the only
adapter call. No later request establishes that the model received or used
that record. Visual or trace continuity therefore must not be read as
postcommitment model uptake.

Minimum warranted correspondence:

> The same bounded early-availability advantage appeared when the disclosed
> discriminator was an action binding and when it was a receipt of an
> apparatus-established consequence. This supports a shared predecision
> discrimination pattern, not temporal duality or a recurrent actor process.

## 7. Terminology Boundary

| Term | Bounded operational meaning | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| observation boundary | exact content included in or excluded from the serialized predecision policy request | not biological perception or a universal epistemic boundary |
| commitment | the accepted terminal selection that fixes scoring in these fixtures | not a measured deliberation threshold or a separate pending phase |
| consequence | the authoritative symbolic RED / BLUE value produced or established by the fixture | not a material-world effect or a generalized consequence system |
| receipt | an explicit trace record carrying an acceptance result or fixture-established world value | acceptance and consequence receipts remain distinct; a late trace event does not prove model reception |
| coordinate | a named experimental factor or recorded value | no intrinsic coordinate system, independence, metric, or transformation law |
| surface | a particular input representation or permitted action interface | no continuity, dimension, or geometric structure |
| semantics | the declared task and action-contract interpretation | operational contract semantics only; no internal or general semantic competence |
| dynamics | ordered fixture events and conditional transitions | no learned evolution law, autonomous trajectory, or general temporal model |

`Behavioral decision surface` may name only a finite,
realization-specific condition-to-action table. `Geometry`,
`observation-decision-consequence geometry`, `temporal cognition`, `agency`,
`memory architecture`, `coordination architecture`, and `consequence engine`
are not licensed descriptions of this result.

## 10. Method Verdict

**`CHECKPOINT_AND_DWELL`**

The lineage has established that representation and predecision availability
can matter under the retained coupled apparatus, and that authoritative fixture
relations can change consequences without changing selection. More symbolic
permutations risk adding cells without sharpening that conserved result.

Dwell should identify a concrete consequential ambiguity that the current
apparatus avoids by centrally supplying ground truth, association, and
acceptance. It must not let a projected participant count or architecture
select the pressure. No new experiment is selected or authorized by this
checkpoint.
