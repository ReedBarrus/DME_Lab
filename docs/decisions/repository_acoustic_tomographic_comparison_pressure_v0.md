# Repository-Acoustic Tomographic Comparison Pressure v0

## Scope and Starting Boundary

This pass asks whether the independently earned repository and acoustic regimes
share any nontrivial structure without erasing their different evidence and
residue. It reuses committed traces, decisions, registry entries, and
hardware-free tests. It emits no audio, accesses no audio device, adds no
source, and creates no production machinery.

Refreshed local `main` and `origin/main` were identical at
`e75b4a9432abae280d91c5da2a38972c3480ea3b`
(`Second Acoustic Exploration`), with divergence `0/0` and a clean worktree.
The baseline suite passed 469/469 with `python -m unittest discover -s tests`.

The decision record is the interpretation surface. Original traces and tests
remain evidence; no comparison trace or new authority was created.

## Independently Earned Local Structure

Repository evidence was already locally saturated before acoustic acquisition.
The comparison uses, principally:

- repeated equivalent filesystem snapshots at distinct observation intervals
  (`repo_transition_pressure_v0`, D-0016);
- authoritative records and derived surfaces recovered after coordinator
  destruction/recreation (`foreground_persistent_coordinator_pressure_v0`,
  D-0042);
- raw observation, authoritative history, reconstruction, and projection kept
  distinct (D-0020, D-0026, D-0027);
- three real Git command failures represented by null fields plus errors, then
  structurally admitted without source-success certification
  (`degraded_git_admission_pressure_v0`, D-0046);
- the repository horizontal capstone's explicit saturation and ignorance
  surfaces.

Acoustic local structure was earned later and independently in three stages:

- a physical command/capture entry specimen;
- a predeclared five-per-condition primary replication block;
- a new-order recurrence block after a fresh process/backend initialization.

Across the two primary acoustic blocks, both microphone channels yielded:

```text
S1 vs C0 -> not locally discriminable
S2 vs C0 -> locally discriminable
S1 vs S2 -> locally discriminable
```

S2 medians changed between blocks while this verdict pattern recurred. Raw PCM
was intentionally destroyed before ingest; trial identities, requested
conditions, derived measurements, hashes, errors, and timing metadata survived
in raw/nested reconstructed observations.

## Three Correspondence Layers

1. **Source-domain correspondence** compares repository observations with
   acoustic observations before their common DME handling. This is where a
   tomographic claim would need independent substance.
2. **Pipeline correspondence** records that both sources were deliberately
   passed through the same envelope, comparator, ledger, reconstruction, and
   projection code. Similarity here is real but mechanically inherited.
3. **Interpretation correspondence** compares claims made after derivation,
   such as occurrence non-collapse or admission not implying truth. These may
   be useful without establishing source-domain equivalence.

A pipeline invariant is not promoted into source-domain tomography.

## Transformation-Compatibility Audit

### Candidate A

```text
T_repo  = recapture an unchanged observed filesystem configuration
T_audio = repeat capture under an equal requested-condition descriptor
phi     = retain descriptor equality and occurrence inequality
```

The relation “equal descriptor does not collapse distinct occurrence” survives
in both regimes. The square is only partial: repository equality is measured
structural content under a declared snapshot scope; acoustic equality is the
requested C0/S1/S2 command condition, not equality of the physical room or
emission. Calling the square commuting would erase that mismatch.

### Candidate B

```text
T_repo  = destroy coordinator, reopen ledger, derive old history
T_audio = end process, initialize new backend, acquire new trials
phi     = proposed mapping from continuity to recurrence
```

The proposal fails. `T_repo` conserves old durable record identity;
`T_audio` creates new observations and only later derives a matching verdict.
There is no evidence-preserving `phi` between those conserved objects.

### Candidate C

```text
T_repo  = one of several repository-local realization changes
T_audio = fresh acquisition block with shifted numerical measurements
phi     = quotient concrete results to a selected relational summary
```

Each side supports its own quotient-like description, but the selected
relations differ. This is a post-hoc meta-level partial correspondence, not a
tested cross-domain transformation law.

### Candidate D2

```text
T_repo  = repository envelope -> common DME pipeline
T_audio = acoustic envelope   -> common DME pipeline
phi     = identity on the common envelope/record/reconstruction interfaces
```

This compatibility is supported but inherited by construction. It establishes
that the generic pipeline did not invalidate either source regime. It predicts
software behavior from the implementation, not source-domain behavior.

### Candidate E

The common interpretive prohibition—structural admission cannot certify a
stronger source-world claim—survives. The evidence does not form a full
transformation-compatible pair because only the Git side has independent
ground truth of failed source acquisition.

## Independence and Contamination Audit

- Repository distinctions and horizontal findings predate the acoustic regime.
- The initial acoustic conditions came from a local channel-command specimen,
  not from a selected D-number or desired repository correspondence.
- Acoustic replication froze its measurement and rule before acquisition and
  explicitly deferred registry comparison until after its local result.
- Acoustic recurrence froze the prior acoustic verdict pattern, not a
  repository relation. Absolute S2 magnitude was allowed to vary.
- The operator-reported video-contaminated block was retained but excluded from
  the primary acoustic adjudication and is not used in this comparison.
- Occurrence preservation and structural admission were already properties of
  the generic DME method. This weakens independence for Candidates A and E and
  is stated rather than hidden.
- All post-ingest similarities in Candidate D2 are mechanically influenced by
  the fact that both sources use the same implementation. They are classified
  as pipeline correspondence only.
- No acoustic trial was chosen or rerun to reproduce D-0016, D-0042, D-0046,
  “tomography,” or this matrix.

The acoustic local verdict has adequate lineage independence for comparison,
but the strongest apparent software-shape similarity is pipeline-induced.

## Predictive-Power Pressure

No source-domain correspondence currently predicts a fact not already used to
construct it. Candidate D2 predicts that another structurally valid envelope
can traverse the generic pipeline, but that follows from the common software
contract and is not a cross-domain prediction.

No smallest new physical or representational transformation is justified by
this comparison alone. Microphone movement, a new metric, new source, denser
sampling, or retained PCM would each introduce a new basis before a concrete
discriminating prediction has been earned.

## Horizontal Saturation Judgment

```text
freeze_comparison_and_seek_new_transformation
```

“Seek” does not authorize a transformation. It means this comparison should
remain frozen until a future, independently justified transformation has a
predeclared falsifiable expectation capable of separating Candidates A or C
from generic repetition. The present pass does not identify such a
transformation strongly enough to execute or privilege one.

The strongest warranted conclusion is narrower than tomography: heterogeneous
repository and acoustic evidence can coexist in the same generic pipeline
without semantic inflation, and both locally resist collapsing occurrence,
derived representation, or structural admission into stronger claims. The
source-domain correspondences remain partial because their scales,
transformations, and residues do not align completely.
