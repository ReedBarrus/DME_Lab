## 8. Candidate distinction exposed

This specimen motivates, but does not yet establish as a general law:

```text
reconstruction reduction
!=
total effort reduction
```

A continuation surface may displace attention from historical reconstruction into current frontier work rather than cause an investigator to stop earlier.

A future method should therefore observe separately where possible:

- historical reconstruction burden;
- packet production burden;
- packet admission burden;
- duplicated reconstruction;
- current-task adjudication;
- frontier expansion / newly inspected consequential residue;
- novel consequential distinctions recovered;
- total wall time / tool cost;
- correctness and boundedness.

The candidate interpretation is:

> continuation leverage may appear as reconstruction displacement rather than wall-time reduction.

That remains a hypothesis generated by this specimen, not an earned general mechanism.
