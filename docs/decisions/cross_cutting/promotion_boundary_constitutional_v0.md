# Promotion Boundary Constitutional Amendment v0

**Artifact function:** constitutional amendment / cross-cutting design constraint  
**Scientific standing:** NONE  
**Runtime implementation authority:** NONE BY THIS ARTIFACT  
**Promotion authority for any specific candidate:** NONE BY THIS ARTIFACT

## Amendment

The DME operational development process now preserves:

```text
DISCOVERY HISTORY
!=
INTEGRATION SURFACE
!=
DURABLE SUBSTRATE
```

as the first machinery-promotion boundary in the Operational Constitution Seed.

## Operational basis

The branch-retention work demonstrated a concrete repository analogue:

```text
COMMIT HISTORY
!=
BRANCH NAME

EVIDENCE RETENTION
!=
REF SPRAWL
```

Unique history could remain reachable while the intentional navigation surface
was compressed to a bounded durable set. That operational pressure motivates a
broader development rule: preserving discovery history does not require making
the entire discovery apparatus part of the durable runtime substrate.

## Burdens by layer

### Discovery history

Preserve enough causal archaeology to recover:

```text
what was tried
what failed
what was observed
what was adjudicated
what scars were earned
what alternate paths existed
```

Historical preservation does not itself make an artifact a supported dependency.

### Integration surface

Identify the exact bounded behavior proposed for portability.

A valid integration surface should carry only what is required to conserve that
behavior, including where appropriate:

```text
minimal implementation
minimal supporting tests / checks
claim ceiling
lineage references
known retained scars
```

The integration surface is not the experimental history and is not yet durable
substrate merely because it exists.

### Durable substrate

Promotion into durable substrate means downstream machinery may now depend on
the promoted bounded behavior.

That dependency consequence is stronger than historical preservation or
integration preparation and must therefore be explicit.

## Integration rule

```text
PORT EARNED BEHAVIOR
+
PORT MINIMAL SUPPORTING TESTS / CHECKS
+
PORT LINEAGE REFERENCES

DO NOT IMPLICITLY PORT
THE ENTIRE EXPERIMENTAL CAUSAL TRAIL
AS RUNTIME SUBSTRATE
```

## Lineage consequence

A durable primitive should preserve enough lineage to recover why it exists
without requiring ordinary consumers to reenact its full experimental history.

```text
LINEAGE REFERENCE
!=
LINEAGE REPLAY REQUIREMENT
```

This consequence is retained here as part of the constitutional amendment. It
is not separately promoted into the distinction registry by this artifact.

## Non-claims

This amendment does not establish that any current Atlas, Concordance, or other
candidate is ready for durable-substrate promotion.

It does not authorize a merge of any experimental branch.

It does not erase, rewrite, or downgrade historical evidence.

It does not convert operational lineage into scientific standing.
