PURPOSE:
Test whether the exact horizon-scoped hot-retention requirement earned by G7 is
conditioned by the currentness of that exact declared horizon.

This pressure asks:


or compute a quantitative cost optimum.

## Claim ceiling

G8 may establish only that the exact G7 horizon-scoped hot-retention requirement
is currentness-conditioned for the exact declared horizon:
CURRENT preserves the requirement, NONCURRENT removes it for that horizon only,
and UNRESOLVED preserves unresolvedness.

It does not establish global cooling, deletion permission, depreciation,
retirement, quantitative cost optimization, global ecology retention, method
improvement, capitalization, planning authority, execution authority, or
scientific standing.
