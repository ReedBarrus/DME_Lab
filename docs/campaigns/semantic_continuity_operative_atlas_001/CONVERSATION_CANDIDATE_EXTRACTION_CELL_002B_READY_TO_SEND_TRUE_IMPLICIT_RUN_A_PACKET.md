recognize.

historical residue after the incarnation that carried it ends.

ends.

continued worship, an artifact preserving the old form, a city whose
covenant still depends upon it, or enough cognition having been
externalized into a persistent sphere.

In such a case, the new incarnation can continue developing while the
older symbolic form also remains active in the world.

The new incarnation can later encounter that older symbolic form.

classified as identity, succession, inheritance, duplication,
