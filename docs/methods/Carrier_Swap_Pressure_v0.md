# DME_Lab — Carrier Swap Pressure v0

## Status

**PROBATIONARY CANDIDATE METHOD — NOT YET ADOPTED.**

This document proposes a reusable experimental pressure pattern for testing
whether a declared carrier difference is behaviorally material while a
predeclared consequential payload relation is preserved.

Its authority is methodological only.

It does not:

- establish representation invariance;
- establish that semantics are independent of carrier;
- explain why a carrier effect occurs;
- authorize a carrier swap merely because the method exists;
- promote any positive or null result into general architecture;
- make "same meaning" available for post-hoc redefinition.

The candidate earns promotion only if a frozen specimen can execute without
leaving a reviewer room to rescue either outcome by redefining payload identity,
carrier scope, held coordinates, discriminator, or scoring after the result.

---

## Purpose

Use Carrier Swap Pressure when the question is:

> Is downstream behavior tracking only the relation we say matters, or is the
> declared carrier/container treatment itself contributing behavioral effect?

The minimal pattern is:

    freeze content identity C
    freeze held coordinates H
    freeze discriminator T
    freeze scoring

    vary exactly one carrier coordinate K

    observe T

For the first probation specimen, content identity is exact consequential
payload bytes.

The method does not claim the realized model context is identical across
conditions. Any wrapper-induced change in tokenization, parsing, positional
salience, section boundaries, or attention is part of the carrier effect under
test.

Therefore:

    exact payload bytes preserved
    !=
    identical realized model context

---

## Core discriminator

The first bounded positive inference is:

    exact consequential payload preserved
    +
    one declared carrier coordinate changed
    +
    predeclared behavior changed
    →
    this tested carrier difference was behaviorally material

The bounded null inference is:

    no qualifying behavioral difference observed
    →
    behavior was robust to this tested carrier perturbation
    at the predeclared discriminator and threshold

Neither outcome licenses:

    global representation invariance
    carrier irrelevance in general
    semantic equivalence beyond the frozen criterion
    an explanation of why behavior changed
    a claim that all carrier coordinates matter

---

## Preconditions

Before execution, freeze:

1. **C — content identity**
   The exact relation or payload that must remain invariant.

2. **K — carrier coordinate**
   The one representation/container dimension allowed to vary.

3. **H — held coordinates**
   Task wording, authority, evidence set, ordering outside K, invocation
   surface, response contract, run policy, and scoring.

4. **T — behavioral discriminator**
   The exact observable whose change or preservation will be scored.

5. **Equivalence rule**
   A mechanically checkable criterion proving C is preserved before any
   behavioral outcome exists.

6. **Scoring and repetition rule**
   The exact mapping from realization outputs to T and the threshold for a
   qualifying difference.

If any of these can be redefined after outcomes exist, the specimen is not
admissible.

---

## Procedure

### 1. Freeze content identity

Choose the smallest consequential payload relation that can be checked
independently of model behavior.

For the initial probation class, prefer byte identity over semantic
equivalence judgments.

### 2. Freeze one carrier coordinate

Declare exactly what changes between K1 and K2.

Do not bundle ordering, retrieval, provenance path, tool access, authority, or
task wording into the first carrier perturbation.

### 3. Validate the carrier pair mechanically

Before behavioral execution, verify:

- both conditions contain the exact same consequential payload bytes;
- all declared held coordinates are byte-identical where mechanically
  inspectable;
- only the declared carrier treatment differs;
- the discriminator and scoring rule are already frozen.

### 4. Execute under a frozen run policy

Use the same declared invocation surface for both carrier conditions.

A successfully returned realization remains behavioral evidence even when
malformed or surprising. External administration failure is separate from
realization behavior.

### 5. Score only the predeclared discriminator

Do not promote tone, explanation quality, token count, confidence language, or
other unlisted response features into the primary outcome after execution.

### 6. Preserve the claim ceiling

A positive result establishes only tested carrier materiality.

A null result establishes only tested robustness.

Do not infer mechanism from the result alone.

---

## Anti-rescue gate

Before any behavioral run, an independent reviewer should answer:

> Can either outcome still be rescued after the fact by redefining the carrier,
> the preserved content, a held coordinate, the behavioral discriminator, or
> the scoring rule while remaining nominally compliant with the specimen?

If YES:

    REPAIR_REQUIRED

If NO:

    specimen may be considered runnable

A runnable specimen does not automatically promote this candidate method.

---

## Relationship to other methods

Carrier Swap is distinct from Capability Withdrawal:

    Capability Withdrawal:
    X present → X absent
    asks what hidden dependency X was subsidizing

    Carrier Swap:
    K1 → K2 while C remains frozen
    asks whether the carrier difference itself was behaviorally material

Carrier Swap is also distinct from distinction ablation:

    distinction ablation
    removes represented distinction D

    carrier swap
    retains the declared content relation C
    while changing how C is carried

The methods may be combined only under a separately frozen design.

---

## Probation criterion

Carrier Swap may be proposed for adoption into the methods toolbox only after:

1. at least one specimen freezes C, K, H, T, scoring, and run policy before
   behavioral outcomes;
2. deterministic validation confirms exact C preservation and declared wrapper
   isolation;
3. an adversarial preflight cannot rescue either outcome through post-hoc
   redefinition;
4. the first administratively valid behavioral batch completes under the frozen
   contract;
5. the result, whether positive or null, can be stated without explanation
   inflation;
6. the specimen preserves all evidence needed to reconstruct the intervention.

A positive carrier effect is **not** required for method qualification.
A clean null result can still qualify the method if the experiment genuinely
discriminates the declared question.

Promotion remains an explicit later standing decision.

---

## Method ceiling

Carrier Swap is an intervention pattern, not a theory of representation.

Its durable rule is:

> **Preserve the predeclared content relation, change one carrier coordinate,
> observe only the frozen consequence, and refuse to move the goalposts
> afterward.**
