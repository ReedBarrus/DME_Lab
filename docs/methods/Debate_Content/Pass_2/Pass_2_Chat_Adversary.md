PR-001 establishes that true stasis and an unobserved round trip can have equivalent captured endpoint configurations, and that neither “nothing happened” nor “something happened” is inferable from those endpoints alone. PR-006 remains OPEN and asks for a bounded source-relative change discriminator rather than a general observer architecture.

SURVIVING A POSITION

Candidate A's minimum surviving position is:

Endpoint state and traversal evidence are distinct. A persistent source-relative witness may establish intervening relevant change if its meaning, source, scope, interval association, and validity conditions are sufficient to warrant that inference.

Locally, A requires no consequential-geometry tuple, observer atlas, recoverability calculus, transformation graph, or navigation machinery. Its own Round A advocate reduced A to a persistent source-relative witness and concluded that the Null Candidate could satisfy the same requirement.

The surviving result regime is therefore:

valid, soundly associated advancement → YES;
unchanged without completeness → not stasis; UNRESOLVED;
missing, invalid, or ambiguously associated evidence → UNRESOLVED;
scoped NO only if sufficient completeness, retention, and validity are independently established.
SURVIVING B POSITION

After Codex's adversarial pressure, Candidate B reduces to the same bounded mechanism.

Its strongest remaining formal statement is:

A definite verdict is warranted only when every admitted history compatible with the valid, associated observation agrees on the relevant-change predicate.

Thus:

$$ q(K(w))=\{1\}\Rightarrow YES $$ $$ q(K(w))=\{0\}\Rightarrow NO $$ $$ q(K(w))=\{0,1\}\Rightarrow UNRESOLVED. $$

But Astra's advocacy explicitly states that the histories or compatibility sets need not be enumerated or represented at runtime; a bounded witness contract can implement the same decision rule. It ultimately reports DISTINCTIVE B COMMITMENT: NONE FOUND and EQUIVALENT TO NULL UNDER CURRENT PRESSURE.

Codex's adversary survives that position intact: explicit compatibility machinery is explanatory formal semantics, not an additional local observational coordinate.

NULL POSITION

One bounded persistent source-relative traversal witness is sufficient, provided its local semantics preserve everything necessary for this consumer question:

selected source;
relevant-change predicate;
endpoint interval association;
sound positive interpretation;
coverage/completeness state where negative inference is attempted;
retention/reset/rollover semantics;
missing/invalid/conflicting states.

Null does not require these to become shared observational-geometry primitives. They may simply be fixed or carried by the bounded witness contract.

SHARED GROUND

All three positions currently preserve the same substantive local obligations:

$$ \text{endpoint equality} \neq \text{absence of traversal} $$

and:

$$ \text{witness advancement} \neq \text{complete history}. $$

All three authorize YES from a valid, sound, correctly associated positive traversal witness.

All three refuse to derive NO from unchanged evidence unless the observation basis establishes sufficient completeness and retention.

All three preserve missing, reset, invalid, conflict, and ambiguous association rather than silently coercing them into stasis.

All three require the witness result to remain jointly associated with the selected source, relevant-change predicate, and target interval.

Round A's adversary already identified the substantive condition as separation between histories containing no relevant change and histories containing at least one relevant change; naming additional formal objects does not establish that separation.
