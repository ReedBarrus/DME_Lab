HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS:

A relation is usefully modeled not merely as a statement that two objects are
connected, but as a consequence-bearing orientation/configuration including
relative state, dependency, coupling, load, flow, and preservation/rupture
conditions.

or predicts consequence that endpoint-local descriptions do not.
