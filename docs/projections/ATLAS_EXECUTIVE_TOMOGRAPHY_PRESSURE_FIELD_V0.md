## Projection

Planning should not be represented as one scalar task-ranking function.

Candidate operating picture:

UPSTREAM:
RELATIONAL PRESSURE FIELD

MIDDLE:
SAMPLING / TOMOGRAPHIC OPERATORS

DOWNSTREAM:
DISCRETE CONSEQUENCE MEMBRANES

The upstream field may carry multiple simultaneously relevant coordinates such as:

- functional load
- semantic load
- authority load
- provenance load
- temporal load
- coordination load
- uncertainty
- currentness
- reversibility
- dependency order
- witnessability
- unresolved boundaries

Do not collapse these coordinates into one importance score unless a future
pressure earns such a reduction.

## Tomographic planning operator

Candidate planner behavior:

1. recover the current qualified relational field;
2. choose a bounded view / intervention / slice;
3. pressure or sample one local structure;
4. record the resulting deformation, rupture, preservation, or unresolvedness;
5. update only the affected relational coordinates;
6. compare the changed local frame against the whole qualified field;
7. move attention toward a new pressure region without granting consequence authority.

The selector may be used as one simple attention/consequence aperture.
It is not the whole planner.

Candidate concepts:

- bottleneck: a dependency concentration that constrains multiple downstream frames;
- rupture surface: a relation whose failure changes standing or continuity;
- unresolved exterior: a region not yet licensed for extrapolation.

These names are projection vocabulary only.

## Candidate future pressure family

Do not activate while PLANNING_ECOLOGY_001 remains PARKED_NOT_ACTIVE.

P1 — FIELD SLICE RECONSTRUCTION
Provide one bounded qualified field and two independent local sampling operators.
Test whether each reconstructs the affected relations and unresolved exterior
without pretending its slice is the whole field.

P2 — NONSCALAR DIRECTION DISCRIMINATION
Provide two candidate pressure directions with different load-vector signatures.
Test whether the planner preserves the vector differences instead of collapsing
them to one unsupported scalar rank.

P3 — ENDOGENOUS PERTURBATION
Apply one bounded planner intervention whose expected relational effects are
declared beforehand.
Test whether the post-intervention reconstruction preserves unaffected
coordinates and identifies expected versus unexpected deformation.

P4 — TOPOLOGICAL BASIN REVERSAL
Change one dependency relation while holding target fixed.
Test whether the preferred pressure region changes for relational reasons rather
than because of stale prior routing.

P5 — CONSEQUENCE-MEMBRANE HANDOFF
From one rich pressure-field state, produce one bounded candidate that reaches
the next discrete membrane without granting that membrane's standing.
