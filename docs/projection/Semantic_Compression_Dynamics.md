# Semantic Compression Dynamics

**Status:** PROJECTION
**Scientific standing:** NONE
**Architecture authority:** NONE
**Implementation authority:** NONE

This note records a candidate interpretation emerging from the local-model qualification work.

Repository evidence outranks this projection.

The term **semantic compression** is used here only as shorthand for an observed representational transformation:

```text
richer retained source state
        ↓
bounded symbolic response
        ↓
smaller interpreted state
```

It does **not** establish an internal compression mechanism, cognitive process, latent geometry, or model architecture.

---

## 1. Motivation

The local qualification campaign is beginning to expose a problem more specific than whether a model produces a correct or incorrect answer.

A bounded source packet may contain distinctions involving:

```text
ambiguity
missingness
provenance
scope
identity
status
authority
failure history
unresolved residue
```

A model may produce a shorter, mechanically valid symbolic representation that preserves some of those distinctions while collapsing, replacing, or strengthening others.

The relevant question is therefore not merely:

> Did the model summarize the evidence correctly?

A stronger question is:

> Which consequential distinctions survived the representational transformation, which were lost, and which unsupported distinctions appeared?

---

```text
useful evidence extraction

```


The response also failed to preserve:

```text
successful harness execution
!=
general adapter reliability
!=
semantic success of the system under test
```

This remains evidence about one realized interaction, not a general property of the model.

## 3. Candidate Compression Failure Classes

The current evidence is not sufficient to establish a formal taxonomy.

The following are candidate distinctions worth pressure-testing if future evidence repeatedly requires them.

### Distinction loss

Two or more materially different source states become indistinguishable in the output.

```text
A != B
    ↓
    X
```

### Ambiguity collapse

A source state that preserves multiple admissible interpretations becomes singular or categorically resolved.

```text
{A | B | unresolved relation}
        ↓
        A
```

### Missingness collapse

Unknown, unavailable, unpressured, or absent information is transformed into a positive or negative claim.

```text
UNKNOWN
   ↓
FALSE / NONE / CLOSED
```

### Status substitution

A relational, historical, or structural condition is replaced by a simpler lifecycle/status token.

```text
duplicate identity + unresolved association
                ↓
              OPEN
```

The status may be linguistically plausible while failing to preserve why the source state matters.

### Provenance loss

A claim survives while the basis that licenses or limits the claim disappears.

```text
claim + source/boundary
        ↓
      claim
```

### Scope expansion

A bounded result becomes a broader claim than the evidence supports.

```text
survived these specimens
        ↓
generally reliable
```

### Residue loss

The strongest supported result is retained but explicit unresolved remainder disappears.

```text
bounded result + unresolved residue
               ↓
         bounded result
```

### Unsupported strengthening

The output introduces a more definite state than the source licenses.

```text
ambiguous / damaged / unresolved
              ↓
            CLOSED
```

### Mechanism invention

A representational transformation adds causal or mechanistic structure not established by the evidence.

No retained qualification specimen currently licenses a general mechanism claim.

## 4. Compression Is Not Itself Failure

Any practical coordination system requires compression.

A frontier model, local model, UI, status panel, checkpoint, or human summary cannot expose every retained event at every interaction.

The objective therefore cannot be:

```text
preserve all information
```

A more useful candidate objective is:

> Preserve the distinctions required for the downstream consumer to remain within the evidentiary, authority, and consequential boundaries of the source state.

This suggests that useful compression may be task-relative.

A distinction can be omitted safely for one consumer while being load-bearing for another.

Therefore:

```text
information loss
!=
semantic failure
```

unless the lost information is required by the declared downstream use or boundary.

---

## 5. Candidate Conservation Question

A possible future pressure is:

> What distinctions must remain recoverable across a bounded representational transformation for a downstream consumer to act without silently exceeding the source evidence?

Candidate conserved properties may include, where relevant:

```text
identity
ambiguity
missingness
provenance
scope
authority
temporal applicability
unresolved residue
consequence-relevant relation
```

These are candidate pressure coordinates, not established universal invariants.

---

## 6. Relation to Local Automation

Local automation makes this problem operational.

A worker may transform:

```text
source evidence
→ interpretation
→ task state
→ candidate patch
→ test result
→ repair
→ summary
→ escalation
```

Every transformation is an opportunity for consequential distinctions to survive, disappear, or be invented.

Qualification therefore may eventually need to evaluate not only terminal correctness but transformation fidelity across particular task families.

This does not yet justify a general semantic-conservation layer, evaluator architecture, or new control substrate.

---

## 7. Relation to Coordinate Projection

A future read-only local-automation projection could make these transformations observable without changing their authority.

For example:

```text
source observation
        ↓ interpreted by realization R
candidate symbolic state
        ↓ evaluated
semantic finding
```

Rather than displaying only the final status, a projection could retain the relation between:

```text
basis
transformation
output
evaluation
```

This could allow a human or agent to inspect where a distinction was:

```text
preserved
compressed
dropped
strengthened
or newly introduced
```

Any such visualization must remain downstream of retained evidence.

Animation or graphical transformation must not invent causal structure merely to create a coherent visual narrative.

---

## 8. Multi-Realization Pressure

Once the same frozen specimens are executed against another identified local realization, the Lab may gain a useful comparison:

```text
same source basis
    ├── realization A → representation A
    └── realization B → representation B
```

This could help distinguish among:

```text
specimen-specific failure
realization-specific failure
task-family ambiguity
evaluator-boundary ambiguity
recurring representational loss
```

A model comparison should not be reduced to a scalar quality score.

The useful evidence is the differential preservation or loss of declared distinctions under otherwise comparable conditions.

## 10. Nonclaims

This projection does not establish:

* an internal semantic-compression operator;
* a general law of language-model compression;
* a latent semantic geometry;
* general semantic incompetence of the tested realization;
* universal invariants that every representation must preserve;
* that richer representations are always better;
* that compression should be minimized;
* that semantic preservation can be reduced to a scalar score;
* that DME_Lab has established a general conservation law across representations;
* that the coordinate projection described here should yet be implemented.

The current claim is weaker:

> Local qualification has produced bounded examples in which mechanically valid symbolic responses preserved some source structure while failing to preserve other distinctions required by the specimen. This may provide a useful pressure surface for studying consequential information loss across representation changes.
