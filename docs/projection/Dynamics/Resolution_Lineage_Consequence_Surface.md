# Resolution Lineage and Consequence-Sufficient Frontier

Status: PROJECTION

Scientific authority: NONE
Implementation authority: NONE
Architecture authority: NONE

Purpose:
Compress a candidate development regime emerging from repeated pressure over
projection, continuation, authority, execution, observation, standing, and
symbolic resolution.

This document does not establish that the mechanism is general, complete, or
implemented. Repository evidence and executable pressure outrank this projection.

---

## Core Problem

A system capable of preserving increasingly rich provenance and distinctions
faces an apparent recursion problem:

    pressure
    → expose overloaded distinction
    → refine
    → expose another overloaded distinction
    → refine
    → ...

There is no current reason to expect symbolic factorization to terminate
globally.

Global completion is therefore not required.

The candidate alternative is a moving, pressure-relative resolution frontier.

---

## Candidate Development Law

> Develop at the coarsest presently consequence-sufficient frontier.
>
> Refine only where executable pressure demonstrates that the current
> equivalence is insufficient.
>
> Correct or contract prior standing when new evidence shows that the earlier
> claim exceeded its own warranted scope.
>
> Preserve the transformation lineage so finer distinctions can be reopened
> without reconstructing them from scratch.

In compact form:

    STABILIZE
    → EXECUTE
    → OBSERVE
    → ADJUDICATE

        no wound
          → DWELL

        hidden distinction exposed
          → REFINE

        prior standing overclaimed
          → CORRECT / CONTRACT

    → REPROJECT
    → STABILIZE

---

## Refinement Is Not Correction

REFINEMENT:

    prior representation was sufficient at a coarser consequence resolution,
    but new pressure makes a previously collapsed distinction material.

CORRECTION:

    prior standing exceeded what its evidence warranted within the scope in
    which it was claimed.

Therefore:

    refinement
    !=
    correction

and:

    historical interpretability
    !=
    current validity
    !=
    active projection

The durable target is not monotone truth.

It is:

> monotone recoverability of how standing changed.

## Durable Lineage / Active Frontier

The candidate structure separates:

### Durable lineage

Retains recoverable history of:

- observations
- interpretations
- distinctions
- parent equivalences
- breaker pressures
- refinements
- corrections / contractions
- withdrawn standing
- relevant provenance
- unresolved residue

### Active frontier

A pressure-relative projection containing only the distinctions necessary for
the present consequence horizon.

The lineage may grow monotonically while active complexity remains
non-monotonic.

    lineage richness:
      generally grows

    active projection:
      may expand or contract

    current standing:
      may strengthen or weaken

This allows:

> keep the history rich;
> keep the present small;
> unfold only the part current consequence is pressing.

## Pressure-Relative Equivalence

Two states or distinctions may be treated as equivalent only under bounded
conditions.

Conceptually:

    x ~_(H,B,Q,...) y

where the qualifiers may include:

- H — consequence horizon
- B — admitted pressure / evidence basis
- Q — perturbations actually exercised
- observation / continuation regime where relevant

This does NOT mean:

    x == y globally

It means only:

> no retained distinction between x and y currently changes a material
> consequence within the declared pressure regime.

Absence of a discovered difference is not automatically sufficient.

    unchallenged
    !=
    pressure-tested equivalent

Coarsening therefore remains provisional and reopenable.

---

## Anti-Zeno Rule

Infinite refinability does not imply infinite active burden.

Dwell does not require:

    no further distinction exists

It requires approximately:

> another admissible bounded pass produces no material change to standing,
> consequence potential, or warranted action inside the declared consequence
> horizon.

Possible unresolved distinctions outside that horizon remain residue.

    global factorization:
      potentially unbounded

    local pressure:
      finite

    active state:
      bounded

## Factor / Coarsen / Reopen / Correct

### FACTOR

When cases currently treated alike produce materially different consequences
under pressure:

    same parent equivalence
    + different relevant consequence
    → split

### COARSEN

When previously distinguished children have been sufficiently pressured for
the present horizon and no material distinction survives:

    preserved children
    → provisionally travel under common parent

Coarsening is not deletion.

### REOPEN

When a changed horizon, basis, perturbation, or counterexample makes a preserved
child distinction consequential again:

    parent projection
    → recover lineage
    → reactivate split

### CORRECT

When pressure shows that an earlier claim exceeded its own evidence:

    prior standing
    → contraction / withdrawal

Do not disguise correction as refinement.

## Projection Safety

A projection may reduce immediately available discrimination.

It must not gain consequence capacity merely because constraining structure was
omitted.

Candidate invariant:

> Projection may reduce discrimination; it may not increase consequence
> capacity.

Consequence capacity includes what a downstream consumer may legitimately:

- infer
- claim
- interpret as causal
- treat as standing
- decide
- authorize
- commit
- execute

Examples of forbidden strengthening:

    historical authority
    → current authority

    executor return
    → independently observed consequence

    observation
    → causal attribution

    applicable evidence
    → sufficient warrant

    recommendation
    → adoption

    adoption
    → commitment

    capability
    → authority

    authority
    → current admission

Projection must not manufacture warrant, authority, standing, causality, or
commitment.

## Dependency Preservation

Minimum active context is not minimum symbol count.

An active projection must preserve enough dependency identity and relation that
its claims remain interpretable and reopenable.

However:

    dependency preservation
    !=
    dependency materialization

The active surface need not recursively inline its full provenance graph.

It may externalize dependencies when their:

- identity
- relevant relation
- scope
- provenance path

remain sufficiently recoverable for re-adjudication.

Likewise:

    provenance preservation
    !=
    warrant-structure preservation

A surviving support edge must not become a complete warrant merely because
other contributors were projected away.

---

## State Sufficiency

Endpoint equality does not guarantee future equivalence.

    same projected configuration
    !=
    same consequence sensitivity

Relevant historical residue may alter future response.

Therefore:

    state compression
    !=
    continuation-state sufficiency

A projection may be sufficient for selecting the next action while being
insufficient to survive the resulting continuation.

State sufficiency is therefore horizon-relative.

Candidate question:

> Can two cases agree on every active coordinate while responding differently
> to a relevant admissible continuation?

If yes, the active state is insufficient for that horizon.

---

## Observation Is Part of the Dynamics

Observation is not assumed to be passive.

An observation can alter:

- world state
- available evidence
- epistemic state
- active projection / attention
- reachable action

Therefore:

    observation action
    !=
    observation result
    !=
    observation side effect
    !=
    epistemic update

and:

    information gain
    !=
    decision gain

The system must distinguish information becoming available from that information
becoming active in the current projection.

## Decision and Governance Boundaries

Scientific standing alone does not determine action.

    knowledge state
    !=
    decision policy

Candidate pressure selection separates:

    scientific standing
    + unresolved residue
    → admissible pressure set

    admissible pressure set
    + evaluative frame
    → recommendation

    recommendation
    + appropriate decision standing
    → adoption

    adoption
    + commitment
    + authority
    + admission
    + capability
    → attempted execution

Relevant non-equivalences include:

    affected
    !=
    accountable
    !=
    decision standing
    !=
    execution authority

and:

    evaluative competence
    !=
    decision standing

Evidence may change expected consequences without silently rewriting the
objectives, constraints, or priorities under which those consequences are
evaluated.

    frame application
    !=
    frame revision

Frame revision itself requires warrant and appropriate standing.

## Resolution Provenance

The ledger currently preserves event provenance.

A possible next recovery target is resolution provenance:

    event history
    !=
    interpretation history
    !=
    resolution history

A durable replay should eventually be able to distinguish:

    original observation
    then-current interpretation
    resolution under which it was meaningful
    later pressure that broke that resolution
    refinement introduced
    later correction / contraction
    current projected standing

without rewriting prior records.

The stronger recovery question becomes:

> Can the system reconstruct why the current projection has the shape it does?

not merely:

> Can it reconstruct the serialized past?

## Candidate Ledger Pressure

Small bounded specimen:

1. retain an original observation O
2. retain interpretation X under resolution R1
3. introduce pressure P that breaks R1
4. refine into X1 / X2 under R2
5. introduce evidence E that contracts X2
6. replay the full append-only history
7. derive a current projection

Pressure assertions:

    historical claim retained
    !=
    current standing

    refinement retained
    !=
    correction

    correction
    !=
    historical deletion

    coarse parent retained
    !=
    active parent sufficient

    dependency reference retained
    !=
    complete warrant

    historical authority retained
    !=
    current authority

    current projection
    !=
    full historical ledger

The pressure should test whether projection can silently convert historical,
withdrawn, unresolved, or coarsened state into stronger current consequence.
