# DME_Lab — Bounded Research Warrant

!! Not Authoritative !! Still needs to be pressured into legitimacy -- Just a TOOL

**QUESTION**  
[The specific uncertainty this investigation is authorized to address.]

**WHY NOW**  
[Existing evidence, consumer demand, contradiction, or missing observation that gives this question standing.]

**STARTING EVIDENCE**  
[Repository/ref and relevant contracts, implementations, decisions, tests, or traces.]

Treat current repository evidence as authoritative. Preserve the scope and provisional status of prior findings.

**TYPE OF INVESTIGATION**  
Choose the applicable basis:

- **Exploratory:** Determine what is observable or discriminable. Declare the observation boundary and criteria for evaluating the instrument. Treat discovered patterns as candidates.
- **Confirmatory:** Test a specified relation under a specified transformation. Declare the prediction and failure criterion before execution.

Moving from exploration to confirmation requires a separately declared test. Evidence used to formulate a hypothesis does not independently confirm it.

**PERMITTED INTERVENTIONS**  
[What may be inspected, created, changed, executed, captured, or externally affected. Include resource limits where relevant.]

**FORBIDDEN PROMOTIONS**  
[Claims or scope expansions this investigation cannot license.]

Do not promote implementation into evidence, interpretation into observation, or a bounded finding into a general guarantee.

**AUTONOMY**  
Own experimental design, implementation, debugging, and verification within this warrant. Make routine reversible choices without repeated approval.

Change hypotheses when evidence requires it. Build only the instrumentation needed to discriminate the question. Preserve unexpected and negative results.

**EVIDENCE EXPOSURE**  
If using a consumer or reviewer, declare its visible evidence, withheld evidence, and prior context. Record its interpretation and proposed action before revealing withheld evidence.

State whether separation is procedural or enforced. Do not claim independence merely from different role or model names.

**QUESTION-CHANGE RULE**  
Stop dependent work and report when progress requires a different research question or an intervention outside this warrant. Identify what changed, why it matters, and the smallest additional warrant needed.

**STOP CONDITIONS**  
Stop when the question has a supported bounded answer, a counterexample, or a demonstrated evidence limit.

If results remain inconclusive, state what remains unresolved and why further work within this warrant would or would not discriminate it. Do not manufacture additional activity to produce a positive result.

**DURABLE OUTPUT**  
Preserve enough for another participant to reconstruct and challenge the conclusion:

- question and experimental basis;
- competing interpretations;
- executed intervention and evidence;
- result, remaining alternatives, and limits;
- files changed and verification performed.

Keep candidate findings distinguishable from adopted repository conclusions. Follow the agreed review and integration boundary.
